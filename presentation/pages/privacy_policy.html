Privacy Policy

We provide this Privacy Policy to explain how we may collect, retain, process, and transfer your Personal Data when you use our App (SplitIt!) or visit our Site.

Please contact us if you have questions regarding our privacy practices which are not addressed in this Privacy Policy.

What Personal Data Do We Collect?

When you use our App, we will collect Personal Data as necessary to offer and fulfil the services you request. If you want to use the full functionality of the App, you need to sign up. For the sign in an email and a password is required. For your personal profile you can set any arbitrary name and a profile image, optionally. You can create Groups in our App by providing us Personal Data including the name of the Group, the date, as well as Bills and amounts of payments and expenses you and other Group members add to the Group. Groups can be created just for yourself, or you can share a Group with others. When you share a Group with others, the Group data is available to all Group members. You can invite Users to your Group by sending them a secret access code. You share your username and your profile image with your Group Members.

All data you enter into the App is transmitted and stored securely on our servers. This includes your profile data and the Group data. This is necessary to provide you the full service of the App.

Why Do We Retain Personal Data?

We retain Personal Data as necessary to provide the full service offered by our App, to fulfil our legal or regulatory obligations, or to pursue our legitimate interests. You are not required to provide us with your Personal Data; however as a result, you may not be able to use the full service offered by our App. We retain Personal Data for the time necessary for the respective purpose.

Do We Share Personal Data?

We may share your Personal Data with others to provide the services our App offers. We may share Personal Data with third-party service providers that perform services and functions at our direction. These third-party service providers include providers needed to operate our cloud-based infrastructure, databases, and other information technology.

We may also share your Personal Data with others if we need to do so to comply with a law, legal process or regulations.

How Do We Use Cookies and Tracking Technologies?

We do not use tracking technologies.

What Privacy Choices Are Available To You?

You may opt to render some of your Personal Data anonymous, by, for example, using abbreviations or code words instead of other Group members' names. In addition, you may enter expenses without providing an expense title.

You generally can review and edit your Personal Data by using our App and updating the information directly, or by contacting us.

What Are Your Rights?

Subject to limitations set out in EEA data protection laws, you have certain rights in respect of your Personal Data. In particular, you have a right of access, rectification, restriction, opposition, erasure and data portability. Please contact us if you wish to exercise these rights.

How Do We Protect Your Personal Data?

We undertake technical and administrative security measures intended to provide reasonable protection for your Personal Data against loss, unauthorised access, disclosure, and alteration. The security measures include data encryption and access authorisation controls. While we are dedicated to securing our systems, you are responsible for securing and maintaining the privacy of the access codes to your Groups.

Please be aware that, although we endeavor to provide reasonable security for information we process and maintain, no security system can prevent all potential security breaches.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. We will notify you of any changes to our Privacy Policy by posting the new Privacy Policy here. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.
